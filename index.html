Power optimization

Less heat where the laser comes back too fast

Feature spotlight

On a short hatch vector the laser returns to its last line after a fraction of a millisecond, while the metal is still hot. The power optimization gives every hatch vector its own power, computed from the time until the laser comes back.

  • Lower power on short vectors, full power on long ones
  • Optional segment splitting: the power changes along every line
  • Follows each laser's real timeline, also on multi-laser machines

Volumetric optimization

Laser power that knows what lies below the layer

Close to a downskin surface there is less solid metal below the layer to carry the heat away. The voxel-based optimization turns the part into voxels, measures how far every voxel is from the closest downskin surface and lowers the laser power where the heat cannot escape.

  • A depth value for every voxel instead of one downskin area per layer
  • Continuous power along the vectors or automatic discrete zones
  • Works on top of the 2D power optimization

From part to machine

The buildstyle sits between your design and your machine.

  1. Design and prepareModel, orient and support the part in Autodesk Fusion.
  2. Open LPBF BuildstyleContours, hatching, supports, ordering and laser powers in Advanced Toolpath Utility.
  3. ExportLSR, CLI+ or 3MF toolpath package for the build job.
  4. Machine controlRun the job on your machine, for example with the Autodesk Machine Control Framework.
